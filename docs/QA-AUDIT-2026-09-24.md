# QA audit — 24 September 2026

Scope: account creation, KYB, marketing website, templates and tenant websites, platform admin, consent, footers, multi-page structure, Insights and author profiles. Every item below was checked in the running build against the isolated test database; fixes are covered by automated suites (`test:sites`, `extensions-test`, `test:store` and the existing suites).

## 1. Account creation — pass

The registration form asks only for full name, email, phone, password and password confirmation, plus a required Terms and Privacy acceptance that is stored as a versioned consent record. No changes were needed.

## 2. KYB — fixed (was: failed)

**Found:** customers typed their business name freely; the CAC number was checked only by manual staff review, and nothing fetched the registered name.

**Now:**
- The customer chooses the registration type (RC, BN or IT) and enters the CAC number. The server looks it up on the CAC registry through Dojah (`lib/kyb.ts`) and shows the registered name, registration date, status and address in a locked, read-only field.
- The customer confirms authority; the server records the name it fetched itself (a name sent by the browser is ignored). Active entities are verified automatically; inactive or struck-off entities go to compliance review.
- If the registry is unreachable, the customer can send the number for manual review; staff record the registered name.
- Configuration: `DOJAH_APP_ID`, `DOJAH_SECRET_KEY`, optional `DOJAH_BASE_URL` (`https://sandbox.dojah.io` for sandbox keys). `KYB_PROVIDER=sandbox` is for automated tests only and must never be set on a live platform.

## 3. Consent and terms

| Form | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registration | Terms + Privacy required, versioned | Unchanged |
| Marketing contact form | No consent | Required checkbox linked to the Privacy notice; server rejects without it and records `consented_at` |
| Tenant enquiry form | Consent referenced "the footer" | Links directly to that website's privacy policy |
| Store checkout | No consent | Required acceptance of the store's own terms of sale, refund policy and privacy policy (linked); enforced on the server and stored with the order |
| KYB | Generic checkbox | Explicit authority + CAC verification consent |

A cookie consent banner is **not** required: the platform and customer websites use only strictly necessary storage (sign-in session, cart, unsaved-draft backup). This is now documented in the Cookie policy. If analytics or marketing tools are ever added, a consent banner must be introduced first.

## 4. Marketing website

**Pages:** multi-page (home, templates, features, pricing, about, contact, how it works, help, FAQ, insights listing and articles, author pages, legal pages).

**Found and fixed:**
- Only two legal pages existed. Added **Cookie policy**, **Acceptable use policy** and **Refund & cancellation policy** (migration 019, editable in Platform admin → Legal pages). *Have these reviewed by counsel before launch; the refund terms describe current product behaviour.*
- Footer rebuilt to a standard structure: brand statement, socials and sign-up call to action; Product, Resources, Company and Legal columns (legal pages in a fixed order); bottom bar with copyright and legal shortcuts.
- Insights filter used article slugs; it now uses the categories defined in the CMS, shows counts, highlights the active category and hides empty ones.
- Author profile pages show role and professional links.

**Recommendations — implemented 24 September 2026:**
- Testimonials: a Platform admin → Testimonials section. A testimonial cannot be published unless staff confirm the customer's written permission; the homepage section appears only when consented testimonials are published (no placeholders).
- The homepage shows all ten templates with a filter (All / Business websites / Online stores).
- Pricing pages link to the feature comparison and answer billing questions (upgrades, downgrades, cancellation, commission, domains, payment methods), linking the Refund & cancellation policy.

## 5. Templates and tenant websites

**Multi-page:** every new website is provisioned with its industry's core pages (e.g. About, Practice Areas, People, Contact), the industry's legal set (terms, privacy, cookies plus refund/shipping/booking/medical disclaimer as relevant), Shop/Cart/Checkout for stores, and — on Growth and Advanced — Insights, now added to the navigation automatically.

**Found and fixed:**
- Template previews were a single scrolling page with `#` legal links. Previews are now multi-page: Home, the industry's core pages, Insights listing and sample articles, every legal page and, for stores, a Shop page. All navigation, footer and button links go to real preview pages.
- Store template previews showed no products; they now show a sample product row and Shop page.
- Homepage **Latest insights**: blog-enabled websites show the three newest published articles automatically (unless the owner placed or hid an Insights section).
- **Insights listing** with category filter chips from the subscriber's own categories (with counts and active state); categories are now published for corporate websites too.
- Articles have breadcrumbs, category link, linked byline and an **About the author** card; author pages list the author's articles.
- Collection listings showed raw HTML in excerpts; fixed, with image cards.
- A leftover "EXPLORE" grid of every page was removed from homepages.
- Footer: contact rows with icons; verified CAC number shown as a trust signal; "Website by Omnyvox" pointed at the tenant's own homepage and now links to the platform.

## 7. Platform admin

**Found:** records were oversized cards dumping raw database fields (`subscription_site_id`, ISO timestamps, `true`/`false`); no search, filtering or pagination (140+ websites in one list); the "Save plan" button sat above two fields it saves; a marketing-style heading; CMS categories were free text; no author fields; text areas could be resized.

**Now:**
- One reusable data table (`components/admin-table.tsx`) for every list: search, status filter, record count, 25-per-page pagination, formatted dates and amounts, colour-coded status badges, responsive stacked rows on phones.
- Row detail panel for actions (support replies, KYB review with registry details, domain activation, merchant approval, account/subscription/payment facts).
- CMS: category chosen from defined categories, author name/role/links/photo, delete from the edit dialog.
- Sticky navigation and full-height layout; overview without marketing copy; plan save button at the end of the form.

**Implemented 24 September 2026:**
- **Email delivery** monitor: every outgoing message with status (queued, retrying, sent, failed), attempts, last error and provider reference; failed messages can be retried. Message bodies are never shown because they can contain sign-in codes.
- **Account actions:** disable sign-in with a recorded reason (all sessions end immediately and the person is told the account is disabled), restore sign-in, and resend a verification code. Staff cannot disable themselves; only a super administrator can disable a super administrator. The accounts list shows sign-in state, two-factor status and last sign-in.

## 8. Text areas

No text area anywhere can be resized by dragging (`resize: none` globally). Text areas grow with their content instead (`field-sizing: content`, capped at 60% of the viewport height with scrolling).

## 9. Branding

Omnyvox purple, typography and the logo are consistent across the marketing site, workspace and admin. Footers carry the Nexoris attribution; tenant websites carry their own brand with a discreet platform credit.
